  • email addresses
  • usernames
  • passwords
  • contact or authentication data

Sensitive Information. We do not process sensitive information.

Payment Data. We may collect data necessary to process your payment if you choose to make purchases, such as your payment instrument number, and the security code associated with your payment instrument. All payment data is handled and stored by Apple Store In-App Payments and Google Play In-App Payments. You may find their privacy notice link(s) here: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not directly collect or store any payment card details. The information you provide for a purchase is sent directly to these processors, and we only receive a confirmation token or receipt to validate the transaction. We encourage you to review their privacy policies to understand how they handle your data.

Social Login Data. We offer the option to register or log in using your existing Google or Apple account. If you choose to do so, we will collect certain profile information from that provider (name, email address, and profile photo where available), as described in "HOW DO WE HANDLE YOUR SOCIAL LOGINS?" below. Application Data. If you use our application(s), we also may collect the following information if you choose to provide us with access or permission:

  • Mobile Device Access. We may request access or permission to certain features from your mobile device, including your mobile device’s camera, microphone, storage, and notifications. If you wish to change our access or permissions, you may do so in your device’s settings.
  • Mobile Device Data. We automatically collect device information (such as your mobile device ID, model, and manufacturer), operating system, version information and system configuration information, device and application identification numbers, hardware model, Internet service provider and/or mobile carrier, and Internet Protocol (IP) address (or proxy server).
  • Push & Local Notifications. With your permission (requested on iOS and Android 13+), we schedule daily local practice reminders on your device at approximately 12:00 PM your local time. These reminders are generated entirely on your device and no notification content is transmitted to our servers. You can disable reminders at any time in your device’s Settings → Notifications → WordCards, or from within the app’s Settings screen.
  • Profile Photo. If you choose to set a profile picture, the image you select (from your camera or photo library) is uploaded to and stored in Google Firebase Storage under a path linked to your user ID. The resulting download URL is stored in your Firestore user profile.

Learning data we store on your behalf

In Short: We store your vocabulary progress, test history, and conversation history in our cloud database so your learning data syncs across devices.

The following data is stored in Google Firestore under your user account:

  • Known Words. Words you mark as "known" within any word deck, stored to track your vocabulary progress.
  • Test History. Each test question you attempt, including the question ID, source word ID, whether your answer was correct, time taken, and a timestamp.
  • Answered Questions. A record of questions you have answered correctly, used to avoid repeating questions too soon.
  • Chat Sessions & Messages. Your AI chat history, including session metadata (title, timestamps) and up to 25 messages per session. Older messages are automatically deleted from our servers on a daily basis.
  • Custom Decks. Word decks you create, including deck name, description, color, and the words you add to them.

Usage and quota data stored locally

To enforce free-tier usage limits, we store the following counters locally on your device using your device’s secure local storage. This data is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Number of voice chat sessions used (lifetime and monthly)
  • Number of test questions answered (daily counter)
  • Number of AI chat messages sent (daily and lifetime counters)
  • Number of custom decks created

These counters reset automatically on a daily or monthly basis based on the applicable limit.

This information is primarily needed to maintain the security and operation of our application(s), for troubleshooting, and for our internal analytics and reporting purposes.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes to such personal information.

12. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognizing or honoring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.
