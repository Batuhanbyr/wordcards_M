2. DESCRIPTION OF SERVICE

WordCards is an English language learning application that offers the following core features:

  • Visual Vocabulary Learning. Curated word decks with definitions, example sentences, pronunciation audio, and real-world video clips sourced via the YouTube API.
  • Smart Testing System. Adaptive quizzes and flashcard sessions ranging from CEFR levels A1 to C1, designed to reinforce vocabulary retention.
  • AI Chat Assistant. A conversational AI partner powered by third-party large language model providers that helps you practice English, corrects your mistakes in real time, and engages in topic-based conversations.
  • Push-to-Talk Voice Input. A microphone-based feature that converts your speech to text for use in the AI chat feature.
  • Offline Mode. Downloaded content can be accessed without an internet connection, allowing uninterrupted study on the go.
  • Daily Reminders. Optional local notifications scheduled on your device to remind you to practice each day.

We reserve the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue any feature of the App at any time without prior notice.
